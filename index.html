The Eta Tau Chapter, widely recognized as one of Texas State's most distinguished fraternal organizations, continues to thrive as a leadership-centered brotherhood. Thanks to our dedicated members and loyal alumni, our chapter stands as a model of excellence — in scholarship, service, and character.

Explore this site to discover how Sigma Nu's values are lived out every day on the banks of the San Marcos River.

Character & Purpose

The Eta Tau Chapter is led by a committed Executive Council dedicated to advancing the chapter's mission, fostering brotherhood, and producing graduates who make an impact in their industries and communities.

A Culture of High Standards

Led by Chapter President since November 2025, the Eta Tau Executive Council oversees more than 75 active members. Navigating complex peer dynamics, facilitating difficult conversations, and upholding the institutional integrity of Sigma Nu — our leadership is trained to rise to every challenge.

The Brotherhood

Bonds Built to
Last a Lifetime

Brotherhood is the heartbeat of the Eta Tau Chapter. From academic accountability to lifelong friendship, our members push one another to achieve more than they ever thought possible — and show up for each other every step of the way.

Whether it's navigating the demands of Texas State academics, competing in intramurals, or celebrating each other's milestones, the bonds forged in Eta Tau are unlike any other.
